## Recording Tips

### Tools to Use:
- **OBS Studio** (Free, professional)
- **Loom** (Easy, web-based)
- **Zoom** (Record meeting)

### Settings:
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Audio: Clear microphone
- Cursor: Show cursor highlights

### Best Practices:
1. **Prepare:** Test everything before recording
2. **Script:** Write narration beforehand
3. **Pace:** Speak clearly and not too fast
4. **Focus:** Zoom in on important elements
5. **Edit:** Cut out mistakes and pauses
6. **Length:** Keep under 5 minutes

### What to Avoid:
- ❌ Don't spend time on login/signup (just show it works)
- ❌ Don't show errors or bugs
- ❌ Don't read text on screen
- ❌ Don't go over 5 minutes
- ❌ Don't use poor audio quality
